Review Rules

This is the sorting logic I’d use before updating Notion.

How to decide the correct initiative
  • Ask what outcome the task advances, not which keyword appears in the title.
  • Group by the stakeholder who needs the rollup: Alex, SLT, SA/PM team, Product, or Sales.
  • Keep execution subtasks under the initiative they serve, even when the task uses another system.
  • Use Operations Maintenance only for true upkeep, not for orphaned strategic work.
Generator rule change

The dashboard generator should treat Roadmap Workstream as human-owned. It can backfill blanks and flag suspicious matches, but it should not overwrite reviewed workstreams automatically.
