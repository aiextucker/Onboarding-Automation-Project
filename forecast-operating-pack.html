Monthly Owner Commit Flow

Use this on the first business day of the month before the forecast review. Each owner records their monthly commit once. Later forecast changes should show movement against this original commit instead of rewriting the starting point.

FieldRequired EntryPurpose
OwnerNamed PM, SA, or functional ownerPrevents PM work from being hidden under SA-only views.
Line of businessPSA, Billing, or PaymentsKeeps rollups comparable without blending different lifecycle rules.
Client / projectClient name and link to source recordProvides traceability for SLT questions.
Commit bucketWorst case, Most likely, Best caseLets the meeting talk about confidence, not just dates.
Expected dateActivation, graduation, bill-out, or payment-live dateAnchors weekly movement.
MRR / valueMRR when available; otherwise count-onlySupports Count and MRR views without forcing bad data.
Owner rationaleOne to three sentencesExplains why the owner believes the commit is achievable.
DependencyClient, product, data migration, billing/payment, internal queue, noneTurns movement into a blocker pattern.

Meeting Language

Opening: "This is the frozen first-day commit. Weekly movement should be read as change against this baseline, not as a replacement for it."

Owner prompt: "What changed since commit: date, confidence, blocker, or scope?"

Close: "Anything that moved out needs a reason owner. Anything that moved in needs a confidence note."

Payments Forecast Readout Script

This gives JD and the Payments lane the same concise readout rhythm as PSA and Billing. It should be used as the first draft until Payments has deeper automated movement tiles.

  1. Start with count: "Payments has [count] expected live items this month, with [count] bankable, [count] likely, and [count] at risk."
  2. Name movement: "Since the prior review, [count] moved in, [count] moved out, and [count] changed confidence."
  3. Explain blockers: "The main blockers are processor readiness, underwriting, token/ACH setup, and client test-payment timing."
  4. Call owner action: "The next action is [owner] to [action] by [date]."
  5. End with confidence: "Current most-likely Payments outcome is [count/value], assuming [top dependency] clears."

One-Minute Version

"Payments is currently at [bankable] bankable and [likely] most-likely for the month. The primary movement since last review is [moved item]. The biggest risk is [dependency], owned by [owner]. If that clears by [date], we hold the current commit; if not, the likely outcome drops to [fallback]."

What This Does Not Claim

  • It does not create a new production database.
  • It does not rewrite forecast history.
  • It does not send anything to JD, SLT, or owners automatically.
  • It does not replace source-of-truth client records.

Recommended Next Automation
