How It Works
Scripts are generated live from Notion data — not filled in manually
Click Forecast Readout on any Team Overview card in the SLT Dashboard. The readout is built from the current Notion sync: forecast dates, statuses, blockers, Confidence, milestone progress, owner goals, and historical snapshots. Copy the output and use it to guide the forecast call.

Source fields: Notion PSA Onboarding Clients (PSA) · Notion Billing Onboarding Clients (Billing) · Owner Goals on the Forecast database · dashboard snapshots for movement deltas.

Proposed Forecast Call Flow
Recommendation
Use one short spoken flow and keep movement detail in the dashboard
The proposed change is not to review forecast movement at the individual-owner level each week. The dashboard should keep the snapshots and movement deltas, while the copied/read-aloud portion stays focused on projects that can impact the forecast, key risks, the owner's strategy to keep work in forecast, and any help needed from leadership.

This is the proposed structure for the copied/read-aloud forecast script. Variables are shown as field names so leadership can review the flow without getting caught in placeholder math.

Of those, I committed [Original Monthly Commitment Count] to [Activation or Graduation Verb] this month for [Original Monthly Commitment MRR]. [Completed This Month Count] have [Activated or Graduated Verb] so far for [Completed This Month MRR]. Logic PSA Confidence — How It's Determined

The Confidence shown in the script is not always the raw Notion field value. The script applies an auto-inference rule when the field is blank:

Auto-Inference Rule
If Confidence field is blank AND no blocker is set → treated as High
If Confidence field is blank AND a blocker exists → treated as Medium
If Confidence field has an explicit value → that value is used as-is

This means a client can appear as High Confidence without the field being explicitly set — it is inferred from the absence of a blocker. To override, set the Confidence field explicitly in Notion.

Scenario BucketPSA Confidence IncludedMeaning
Worst Case High Firmest commitments only — no blocker, or blocker is explicitly resolved
Most Likely High Medium Primary forecast number — realistic range
Best Case High Medium Low All non-canceled clients in the month — ceiling, not a commit
Logic Milestone Progress — How It's Shown

Every client line in §2–§4 includes a milestone progress summary: Milestone progress: required X; overall Y. The required figure covers critical-path milestones; overall covers all milestones. These pull from the Notion milestone snapshot.

Milestone completion contributes to the milestone progress percentage shown on the dashboard. The scoring weights each milestone by status:

Milestone Scoring
Completed = 1.0 point — full credit
In Progress = ⅓ point — partial credit
All other statuses (Not Started, On Hold, Blocked, Deferred) = 0 points

Keeping milestone statuses current in Notion directly affects the progress percentage shown in scripts and on the dashboard. An In Progress milestone is worth one-third of a Completed one.

§ 3 Most Likely Additions

Clients with inferred/explicit Confidence = Medium who are not already in the Worst Case section. Same per-client format as §2. These are realistic additions that need a blocker cleared or a date to hold.

§ 4 Best Case / Outside Shots

Clients with Confidence = Low who are not in §2 or §3. These are acknowledged but not counted as commitments. Same per-client format as §2–§3.

§ 5 Replacement / Pull-In Opportunities

Up to 5 clients whose Forecasted Graduation Date falls in the next calendar month and whose Confidence is Most Likely or better (High or Medium). These are candidates to accelerate if a current-month client slips.

Each line includes: current forecast date, MRR, milestone progress, blocker status, and a standard recommendation: "only pull in if required milestones and client data are ready."

§ 6 Not Kicked Off But Forecasted This Month

Clients who have a Forecasted Graduation Date in the selected month but no Kickoff Date set in Notion. These should be validated before being counted in any committed number — a graduation date without a kickoff date is almost always a placeholder.

Each line includes: client name, forecast date, milestone progress, and a fixed recommendation: "validate kickoff status before counting in the committed forecast."

§ 7 Product / Process Blockers / Follow-Ups

A catch-all attention list that aggregates three groups:

  • Any client in the month's forecast who has a blocker set
  • Any client whose Confidence is not High (including auto-inferred Medium/Low)
  • All On Hold and RTS clients for this SA (regardless of forecast date)

Each client line includes: blocker or status reason, milestone progress, Sales rep, days since last interaction, and recommendation. Sorted by forecast date then name.

Recommendation Logic — Auto-Generated
Status contains RTS Sales re-engagement required; keep out of worst/most-likely forecast until movement is confirmed.
Status contains On Hold Confirm re-engagement path or move toward RTS/pre-cancel if no movement.
Blocked + Confidence Low Move date unless blocker clears immediately.
Blocked + Confidence High or Medium Keep date only if blocker has owner + due date.
Confidence Low, no blocker Validate date; move if no clear path this week.
No blocker, not RTS/Hold, not Low Keep forecast date.
Overall Overall PSA Forecast Script

Generated from the Generate Overall Script button in the SA section header on Team Overview. Aggregates all PSA Solutions Analysts into one combined readout — same 7-section structure, with the total goal set to the sum of all individual SA goals. Prior-snapshot movement uses the combined portfolio.

Month selector appears at the top of the modal to switch between forecast months (same months that have owner goals set in Notion).

Billing Forecast Script
4 Sections — What Each One Contains
Key Difference vs PSA
Billing separates activation from graduation
PSA forecast discussions center on graduation. Billing forecast discussions have two tracks: Phase One activation and Phase Two graduation/completion. Phase One activation is the revenue-recognition event for Billing MRR. Billing graduation is later completion hygiene and should not be blended into the same forecast count as activation.

Billing also uses an explicit Confidence field — no PSA-style auto-inference. The Confidence field on each Billing client must be set explicitly to High, Medium, or Low. If it is blank, it defaults to Low (blank) — which means the client only appears in Best Case. Fill the field before using Billing scenario numbers in a commit conversation.

Billing scripts are available to PMs and SAs only. DEs are excluded.
Logic Billing Confidence — Scenario Bucket Rules
ScenarioConfidence Values IncludedBlank ConfidencePurpose
Worst Case High Excluded Floor / must-land number
Most Likely High Medium Excluded Primary forecast call number
Best Case High Medium Low Included — treated as Low Ceiling / capacity planning only
⚠️
Until Billing Confidence is populated for all active clients, scenario buckets are directional only. Clients without a Confidence value appear in Best Case only — not in Worst Case or Most Likely. The script surfaces a count of missing Confidence records in §1 and §3.
Logic Activation vs Graduation Track

Billing clients follow one of two tracks, determined automatically:

Track Determination
If no Activation Date exists → Phase One Activation track — uses Current Forecast Date / forecast activation date
If the client has an Activation Date set → Phase Two Graduation track — uses Current Graduation Date for completion/graduation

The script now separates these into different sections. Activation is the primary Billing forecast number tied to MRR recognition. Graduation/completion is a secondary Phase Two readout.

§ 4 Data Needed Before Reliable Scenario Reporting

This section appears at the bottom of every Billing script. It tells the reader whether Confidence data is complete.

If all active clients have Confidence set: "Billing Confidence is populated for all active Billing clients in this owner view."

If any are missing: Two action lines appear — a PM/SA action item to fill Confidence, and a warning that scenario buckets are directional only until populated.

Guide Facilitator Prompts

Use these during the call to keep readouts honest and blockers actionable:

  • "Any blocker?"
  • "Who owns it?"
  • "By what date does it need to clear?"
  • "Does it threaten the forecast date?"
  • "Is this forecast date real or a placeholder?"
  • "Is the Confidence field accurate?"
  • "Has a kickoff happened yet?"
  • "Does Sales need to re-engage?"
  • "Stay active, RTS, or pre-cancel?"
  • "Is the last interaction logged?"
  • "Is Confidence populated in Notion?" (Billing)
  • "Which activation scenario are we committing to?" (Billing)
  • "Which graduation/completion items are separate Phase Two follow-through?" (Billing)
Close Post-Call Closeout Checklist

Before the next sync, confirm these are updated in Notion:

  • Forecast date changes captured for any clients that moved
  • RTS / On Hold / pre-cancel decisions reflected in status
  • Blockers updated with owner and due date
  • Confidence field set for any clients where it was discussed
  • Billing Confidence populated for any missing clients surfaced in §4
  • Billing activation forecast separated from Billing graduation/completion forecast
  • Month-end placeholder dates replaced with real dates or marked Low confidence
  • Kickoff dates added for any clients surfaced in §6 (Not Kicked Off)
  • Client interactions logged for any stale accounts flagged in §7
  • Milestone statuses updated so progress reflects actual state

Example PSA Script — Jordan M., May 2026

Jordan has 4 clients in view. The readout focuses on projects that can affect the forecast, the risks, the plan to protect the forecast, and where leadership help is needed.

# PSA Weekly Forecast Call — Jordan M. Month: May 2026 ## Forecast Readout I have 6 active onboarding clients representing $14,200 MRR. My original monthly graduation commitment is 3 graduations for May 2026. So far this month, 1 client has graduated representing $2,100 MRR: Prestige Managed Services. Projects that can impact my forecast: - Greenfield Technology is forecast for May 22 at $2,500. Required milestones are 9 of 10 complete, overall milestones are 14 of 16 complete, and confidence is High. The key item to protect the forecast is keeping final launch validation on schedule. - Quick Copper Technologies is forecast for May 29 at $3,200. Required milestones are 6 of 10 complete, overall milestones are 10 of 16 complete, and confidence is Medium. The key item to protect the forecast is clearing the technician contact-permission blocker. Key risks: - Quick Copper Technologies is at risk because technicians are receiving 403 permission errors when creating contacts. I will make a forecast decision if Ray/Engineering does not resolve the blocker by May 24. - Coastal Systems is at risk because it has a May forecast date but no kickoff date. It should not be treated as reliable until kickoff status is confirmed. Strategy to keep them in forecast: - Quick Copper Technologies: I am keeping the date only if the engineering blocker has an owner, due date, and visible progress by May 24. - Greenfield Technology: I am protecting the date by keeping the client on the final validation path and checking launch readiness daily. Help needed from leadership: - Quick Copper Technologies: I need help confirming Ray/Engineering has the permission blocker tracked and can hit the May 24 ETA. - Coastal Systems: I need a keep-or-remove decision if kickoff is still unconfirmed by the next forecast review.
Reading the PSA Example
What to notice
§1Roll-Up tells the story upfront

The opening anchors the discussion in active workload, original commitment, and completed graduations. The dashboard still holds the deeper snapshot and scenario detail.

§2Only Greenfield is a clean commit

One client, High confidence, no blocker, 9/10 required milestones done. The readout explains what must happen to protect the date.

§3Quick Copper is the swing client

Medium confidence because it has a blocker — 403 errors, owned by Ray/Engineering, due May 24. The facilitator should ask: does Ray have this tracked? Is the ETA real?

§4Summit and Coastal are not commitments

Coastal is called out because the forecast date depends on kickoff status. The discussion is about the decision needed, not a separate movement bucket.

PlanStrategy is explicit

The owner states the condition for keeping Quick Copper in forecast and the operating action for Greenfield. That makes the weekly follow-up concrete.

AskLeadership help is visible

The script ends with the exact leadership help needed, so the call produces a decision or action item instead of a passive status review.

Reading the Billing Example
What to notice
§1Activation is the primary commit

Westside and Northgate are the Phase One activation forecast. That is the forecast number tied to recognized Billing MRR. Harbor is kept separate because it already activated.

§2Activation scenarios are separate

§3Graduation is Phase Two

Harbor Digital is already activated, so its May item is graduation/completion. It matters operationally, but it should not inflate the Phase One activation commit or recognized MRR forecast.

§4Northgate is a data action item
