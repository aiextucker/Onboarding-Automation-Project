SLT Takeaway

The PSA conversion issue appears to be less about kickoff timing and more about post-kickoff execution drag. Sold-to-kickoff is relatively tight at a 9-day median, but kickoff-to-graduation stretches to a 105-day median for completed clients. Forecasts are moving later during the month, and the reason mix points to migration/integration readiness as the biggest actionable constraint.

Recommended Action

  1. Owner-validate the top 10-15 May PSA pushes before final attribution.
  2. Separate migration/integration pushes into subtypes: third-party dependency, client data readiness, internal data engineering queue, product/API limitation, and billing/payment setup dependency.
  3. Add a monthly SLT forecast-health footnote: count of pushed PSA forecasts, MRR moved later, and top confirmed push reason.
  4. Treat active PSA forecasts as risk-adjusted until the 55-day forecast median converges toward the 105-day actual graduation median or the active cohort has a confirmed reason to outperform history.
