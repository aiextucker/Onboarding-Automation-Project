AI and Agentic PSA Enablement Plan

Generated June 3, 2026 from Notion meeting context, recent Evan strategy conversations, May baseline reports, and Council Round 1 analysis.

Meeting Objective

Align Sales leadership on how Rev.io should use AI and agentic PSA capabilities to sell a premium outcome, improve deal quality, reduce onboarding drag, and create a tighter feedback loop from Sales promises through onboarding reality.

Executive narrative: the point is not just to add AI to the pitch. The opportunity is to reposition Rev.io PSA as an agentic operating system for growing service businesses, while giving Sales better tools to qualify readiness, set expectations, and hand off implementation-critical context before kickoff.

Sales Leadership Talking Points

Positioning

Sell outcomes, not feature parity

Rev.io PSA should not be framed as a cheaper or newer Tigerpaw alternative. The stronger story is an agentic PSA for operators who need to scale with less administrative drag.

Readiness

Qualify implementation commitment earlier

The $500 minimum created some "try it out" behavior. A premium motion needs a cleaner readiness bar: time, data access, owner attention, and change-management commitment.

Handoff

Use AI to structure discovery

Sales calls already contain the signals onboarding needs: prior PSA, accounting system, RMM, users, dispatch model, payment processor, invoice sequence, SureTax, migration risks, and commitments made.

Demo

Make Revvy match the premium claim

Revvy demos need approved paths that work consistently and show agentic action, not generic chat. The demo should map to each prospect's actual operating pain.

Close the loop: Onboarding should feed Sales a monthly issue/risk digest covering common blockers, product gaps, expectation mismatches, and deals that should have been scoped differently.

Concrete AI / Agentic Plays

Play 1

AI Sales Brief Generator

Generate a Sales-to-Onboarding brief from Salesforce, Fireflies or meeting transcripts, and known client records.

  • Core 8 readiness
  • Sold modules and commitments
  • Integration, migration, and setup risks
  • Missing answers and recommended follow-up questions
Play 2

Deal Readiness Score

Score readiness before closed-won or immediately after close using Core 8 completeness, migration complexity, client owner engagement, role clarity, and Revvy fit.

Play 3

Revvy Use-Case Mapper

Identify 3-5 Revvy workflows for each opportunity, then capture those promised use cases so onboarding can reinforce them.

Play 4

Push-Reason Feedback Loop

Use monthly baseline analysis to show why deals slip after kickoff, then update qualification, discovery, demo language, and expectations.

Play 5

Competitive Intelligence Agent

Benchmark against Halo and other platforms with validated battlecards: where Rev.io wins, where claims need Product help, and what migration/integration story is credible.

Specific Leadership Asks

  1. Agree that Revvy and agentic PSA are primary PSA positioning, not a bolt-on.
  2. Approve a required Sales-to-Onboarding AI brief before kickoff.
  3. Confirm the minimum discovery fields Sales must capture or validate.
  4. Add readiness and risk review to the first 30 days after close, especially for low-readiness deals.
  5. Commit to a monthly review of onboarding push reasons and expectation mismatches.
  6. Sponsor competitive benchmarking, especially against Halo.
  7. Align pricing and minimums with buyer commitment and premium positioning.

Recommended Meeting Flow

  1. Open with the strategic reset: premium, agentic PSA, committed buyers.
  2. Show the operating evidence: kickoff is fast; post-kickoff conversion is the drag.
  3. Explain where AI helps Sales: discovery, qualification, Revvy-specific demos, and handoff.
  4. Ask for operating commitments: required fields, brief validation, readiness review, and push-reason review.
  5. Close with the June pilot: AI Sales Brief + Deal Readiness Score + Revvy Use-Case Mapper.

Watchouts

  • Do not overclaim that AI pre-config alone will fix onboarding duration. The product and UX bottleneck is real.
  • Do not use inferred push reasons as final accountability metrics until owner-validated.
  • Do not let Revvy become a demo-only story. Sales promises must carry through onboarding and value realization.
  • Do not raise minimums without tightening buyer-readiness qualification.
